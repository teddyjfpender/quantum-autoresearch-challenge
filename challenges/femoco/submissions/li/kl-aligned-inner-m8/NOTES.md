# Sparse high keep bit in the folded Li inner alias read

This circuit builds on li/kl-l4factored-m8, the checked-out Li score leader. It retains the parent's four-group split one-hot angle delivery, item one-hot read, folded inner-index read, factored angle erasure, eight-bit outer and inner alias draws, and three-bit lookup block settings. The parent measured 17,755.683 Toffolis per step and 471 peak logical qubits on its local full sliced run. Local runs are checks; the pull-request judge uses an independent seed and determines the standing.

## Mechanism and correctness

The inner alias word contains an eight-bit keep threshold. In the parent's table layout, the high keep bit is nonzero over many of the 64 inner bucket rows. The paired, folded item one-hot read therefore pays nonlinear products for that bit at many leaves. Lever .b uses 285 explicit alias-table witnesses in src/walk/sa_low/li_sparse7_alias.txt. In every square inner table, high keep bit 7 can be nonzero only on rows 3, 4, 5, 18, 25, 31, 33, and 38. The three top padding buckets share a donor, preserving the parent's + item-constant offset mechanism. The data file is included at circuit build time; no solver or network is needed by the judge.

Each table has 64 keep and 64 alt bytes, total 16,384 sampled lanes, and its 58 item counts are an exact floor or ceiling of the pinned ideal count. The trusted evaluator accepts the resulting li-sa-est-v1 estimated rounding class and validates the circuit against the resulting lane map. This rearrangement does change some legal rounding choices: exact estimated-class rounding error rises from about 0.56037 to 1.1358 in the evaluator's units. That is a precision tradeoff within the challenge's stated class, and it is disclosed here. The emitted operation on every sampled lane remains exact for the declared map.

The high-bit support reduction changes the inner read ledger from 845 to 805 Toffolis per copy. There are two copies, so the static step cost falls by 80, from 17,755.5 to 17,675.5, at the same 471-qubit peak. Lever .b is default-off; every recorded circuit retains its previous build string and byte identity.

## Research screens that did not enter the scored circuit

The requested persistent nonlinear angle cache was tested directly. At Q=471, the Li leader's 235 hot slots and three group controls have affine leaf-function rank 238. Removing one hot slot retains rank 238 through the one-hot sum relation; removing two drops the rank to 237. Across the 75 four-group angle transitions, each correction span has rank 42 (three groups times 14 bits). A paired exclusive-group product adds at most two row directions, giving a fixed-hot lower bound of 21 Toffolis per transition, or 1,575 buys per pass. The rankdel q14_1.10 persistent schedule tied the leader at static C=17,755.5, Q=471. The q14_1 variant saved 12 Toffolis but raised the peak to 475, worsening the product. This rules out the tested fixed-hot cache at the current qubit budget; it is not a proof against every possible recoding of the entire hot register.

Lever .a, also included here as a default-off experimental layout, preserves every original integer count and aligns a high alt bit on 20 folded rows. Its separate local full run passed at 17,727.516 Toffolis and Q=471, a 28.167-C improvement over the parent with unchanged rounding error. We attempted to compose .a with sparse high keep bit 7 using exact alias constraints. The best bounded ten-row-exception search proved 279 of 285 joint tables and left six solver-unknown; no joint witness is claimed. Reassigning only deterministic donors in the certified .b tables cannot remove another all-table folded bit product, because every real row has a mismatched partial witness. A selective high-keep-bit release was also screened: the available one-hot reread would cost at least 39 Toffolis per copy before comparison cleanup, above the roughly 37.5-C step break-even for saving one peak qubit. Sign-gauge and index/draw-bit changes failed their early cost or precision screens.

## Validation and result

The pinned Li structural test verifies all 285 table witnesses, the eight permitted high-bit rows, three shared padding donors, and acceptance of the estimated rounding class. The existing small exact-spec gadget plus rejected mutant test passes. The release sa_digests suite passed all 266 recorded circuit digests after both new default-off levers. The 4,096-lane reference evaluator passed .b locally at 17,676.717 Toffolis and Q=471, versus 17,755.884 and Q=471 for the parent on that matched test point. On this branch, the full sliced evaluator passed 524,288 lanes at 17,675.580 Toffolis and Q=471: score 8,325,198.180, with 1,024 batches and zero fallbacks. Against the parent's recorded local full result, this is 80.103 fewer Toffolis (about 0.451%) at the same peak, lowering the score product by 37,728.513. The repository contract check and local PR path policy check passed.
