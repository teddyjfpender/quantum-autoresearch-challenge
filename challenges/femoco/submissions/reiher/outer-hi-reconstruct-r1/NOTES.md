# Reconstructed outer high field on Reiher

## Starting point and mechanism

This candidate composes the existing default-off `.u` outer-field reconstruction with the recorded Reiher rank-scheduled circuit `sparse-high-alias-r1`. That circuit already uses `.d` exact sparse-high inner alias tables, a three-group angle one-hot, a 17-direction rank schedule parked at 14 directions across the Majorana, aligned in-place outer item one-hot, donor-padded inner tables, and eight-bit outer/inner alias draws. The parent judge score is 11,476.446 Toffolis per step at 313 peak logical qubits, product 3,592,127.598.

The `.u` design removes both four-bit `hi` fields from the outer QROM word. After the alias choice, the existing aligned item one-hot recovers `hi = floor((item - 54) / 27)` on square items with CNOT fan-out. On a one-body item `r` in 0..53, the existing `is_ob` flag and item bit five recover `hi = 10 + (r >> 5)`: CNOTs set bits one and three, and a CNOT copies item bit five into bit zero. The square item-hot fan cancels that copy on square lanes, so decoding and inverse decoding use Clifford gates only. The inverse decoder returns the four-bit high field to zero before the item one-hot is erased. Every outer item therefore has the same network address as the parent. The lane map, alias counts, rotations, and circuit behaviour for every recorded knob remain unchanged; Reiher support is active only when the new submission enables `.u`.

## Screens and limitations

With inner and outer block exponents two, the static ledger is 11,476.5 × 313 for the parent and 11,462.5 × 313 with `.u`, a reduction of 14 Toffolis per step at unchanged qubits. The outer alias QROM word falls from 29 to 21 bits, and the decoder costs no Toffolis. This is a small 0.122% score improvement, close to the challenge's 0.1% acceptance margin; it is not the architectural breakthrough sought in the broader research loop.

A rank-schedule screen over 15–40 held directions and several parking budgets found the incumbent `q17_1.14` still gave the lowest product with `.u`. Outer block exponent three lowers Toffolis by 34 but raises peak qubits from 313 to 334, worsening the product. Adding item-folded inner read saves 76 Toffolis but raises the peak to 358, also worsening the product. Deriving the remaining outer flags would save only a few QROM products and require a range comparison or a one-body sign lookup. An exact high-keep/signed-alias witness search was explored, but individual bit supports remain spread across many of the 270 square tables. No additional measured improvement from those directions is claimed here.

The peak is shared by inner PREPARE read, inner compare, both SELECT rotations, and Majorana. The first peak has 135 item one-hot qubits plus 20 transient read qubits and one outer qubit. The SELECT peak has 115 RPREP qubits, 17 held rank directions, 13 inner read residue, nine inner comparison qubits, one outer and one spin-select qubit. A qubit improvement requires coordinated changes to both stages.
